How does Setlarr's escrow protect me?
When you pay for an item, your money is held by Setlarr — not the seller — until you receive the item and confirm it matches the listing. You have 48 hours to inspect. If something's wrong, raise a dispute and we'll review.
What happens if the seller doesn't ship?
Sellers have 48 hours after escrow to ship. If they miss that window, we'll automatically refund your money in full, no questions asked.
How long does verification take?
Most NIN and BVN checks complete in under 5 minutes. ID verification with a photo can take up to 24 hours during business hours.
What are Setlarr's fees?
Buyers pay a 2% escrow fee. Sellers pay a 4% transaction fee on completed sales. No fees on refunded or disputed orders.
Can I meet a seller in person?
Yes — Setlarr supports in-person meet-ups in Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan and PH. Use the in-app handover code so escrow only releases when both parties confirm.
